Platform Terms, Compliance & Rules

1. 100-Day Reward Clearance Cycle

Super Shopping acts as an independent customer promotion and loyalty interface. All Cashback Rewards are processed after the respective merchant's return window closes and final commission reconciles, strictly payable on the 101st day via UPI.

2. Anti-Fraud & Live Audit Compliance

To eliminate fraudulent submissions, claims must feature authentic, in-app live captures of invoice and unboxed items submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Screen captures or returned orders are immediately permanently voided.

3. Merchant Association Disclaimer

Super Shopping is an independent promotional directory and is not directly owned, affiliated, or sponsored by Amazon, Flipkart, or any third-party retailer. Product fulfillment, warranties, and logistics reside solely with respective merchants.

4. Digital Assets & Green Coins

Green Coins represent platform micro-learning loyalty credits strictly redeemable for platform digital library assets. They do not constitute official legal currency or direct demandable bank liabilities.
