Terms of Service & Reward Policies

1. Standard 100-Day Clearance Lifecycle

Super Shopping acts as an independent performance marketing platform. All Cashback Rewards are processed strictly following the respective merchant's statutory return window and commission reconciliation, payable on Day 101 via UPI.

2. Anti-Fraud & Physical Proof Validation

To eliminate fraudulent submissions, claims must feature authentic, in-app live captures of invoice and unboxed items submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Screen captures or returned orders are permanently voided without notice.

3. Merchant Association Disclaimer

Super Shopping is an independent directory and is not officially associated with or endorsed by Amazon, Flipkart, or any merchant. Product delivery, warranties, and logistics reside exclusively with the merchants.

4. Digital Loyalty Assets (Green Coins)

Green Coins represent platform micro-learning loyalty credits strictly redeemable for platform digital library assets. They do not constitute official legal currency or direct demandable bank liabilities.
